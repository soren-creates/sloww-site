Almanac

A quiet record of what's changed, version by version. Sloww is a one-time purchase — every update here is included, and always will be.

Version 1.2

June 21, 2026

Your wrist, your iPad, the same sky.

  • Sloww on Apple Watch. The whole clock face on your wrist — sun, moon, and twilight rings drawn from your watch's own location. Always-On keeps it glanceable, and tapping a complication opens it.
  • Apple Watch Complications. Sloww Sun and Sloww Moon on your watch face: the sun's arc through the day and the next light event, tonight's phase, moonrise and moonset.
  • Made for iPad. A native layout that lets the clock grow to fill the screen. In landscape, the clock and readout sit side by side.

Plus a touch of life in the Guide, a fix for the sunset Live Activity countdown, and a stronger main-screen layout under the hood.

Version 1.1

April 18, 2026

Your nightstand, your lock screen, your pocket, your voice.

  • Sunset Live Activity. Countdown on your lock screen as the sun goes down. Ends after civil twilight.
  • Night Mode. Automatically darkens at sunset and shifts to a mono-red palette at nautical twilight. Gentle on dark-adapted eyes.
  • Celestial Bells. Vibrations and notifications at celestial moments. Choose per event, set lead times, and stay connected to the sky.
  • StandBy Widgets. Three new widgets for your nightstand. The clock keeps watch while you sleep.
  • Ask Siri. Ask Siri about sunrise, moonrise, golden hour, and more. The clock answers without opening.
  • Today's Shape. Ask Siri to tell you about today's shape. A lyrical briefing on the character of the day.
  • Settings Lock. Lock your controls so casual touches don't change your view. Long-press to edit, tap Done when finished.

Plus a redesigned Guide, dozens of polish fixes, and some improvements for edge cases around polar latitudes and location failures.

Version 1.0

March 20, 2026

Your day has a shape.

  • 24-Hour Solar Clock. The full day at a glance. Sun position, rise and set, twilight bands, all computed from your location.
  • Moon Ring. Moon position, rise and set arc, and phase icon on the clock face.
  • Lunar Cycle Ring. A monthly ring showing where you are in the lunar month, with cardinal phase markers.
  • Year Ring. Seasonal markers and solstice/equinox positions. Watch the year turn.
  • Breath Ring. Four breathing patterns — box, coherent, 4-7-8, and extended exhale. A quiet companion.
  • Home Screen Widgets. Small, medium, and large widgets. The clock on your home screen, always current.
