Sun & twilight

The sun, drawn as an arc

Its path runs from first light to last, ringed by the three twilights and golden hour. Read how much daylight is left, when dusk starts, and how the arc stretches and shrinks across the year.

Moon, cycle & season

The moon keeps its own time

Below the sun, a ring follows the moon and tonight's phase. Two slower rings turn with the moon's 29.5-day cycle and the year itself — solstice to equinox, and the long pull between them.

Breath

Room to breathe, at the center

The innermost ring paces your breath through four patterns — box, coherent, 4-7-8, and the long exhale. There when you want it, quiet when you don't.

Night Mode

Kind to dark-adapted eyes

After sunset the clock sinks into a darker palette; at nautical twilight an optional mono-red mode takes over, so checking the time never costs you your night vision. Off, Dark at Sunset, or Dark + Red.

Worth asking

Common questions

Does my location ever leave my phone?

No. It stays on your phone, used only to work out your sun, moon, and twilight times. Nothing leaves the device — no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs phoning home.

What does it cost?

$4.99, once. That buys the whole thing — widgets, Siri, Live Activities, Night Mode, every update after. No subscription, no in-app purchases, nothing held back.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone on iOS 17 or later. The face, the widgets, and the bedside StandBy clock are drawn for every current size.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Sloww is iPhone-only for now — it leans on iOS-only pieces like StandBy, Live Activities, and Siri intents that don't have a clean Android counterpart.

Will it work where I live?

Yes. The maths holds from the equator to the midnight sun, and it handles the polar stretches when the sun doesn't rise or set at all.

Can I see places other than home?

Yes. Search a city or drop in exact coordinates, then save the ones you keep coming back to — the place you're headed next week, or the one where it's already tomorrow.

Will it nag me with notifications?

Only if you ask it to. Celestial Bells stay silent until you switch them on, and even then you set each one — which events, how much notice. Nothing arrives that you didn't choose.
